    About the model

    Architecture
    Dense neural network 13 → 64 → 32 → 16 → 1
    Training data
    Keras Boston Housing, training split (404 tracts)
    Preprocessing
    StandardScaler fitted on the training split
    Target
    MEDV: median owner-occupied home value, in $1,000s (1970s)
    Held-out error
    ≈ $2,800 mean absolute error on the 102-tract test split

    Important limitations

    • Educational only. This is a machine-learning demonstration, not an appraisal, lending decision or market valuation.
    • Historical data. The dataset describes Boston-area census tracts in the 1970s. Values are capped at $50,000 and are in 1970s dollars.
    • Not a property price. Each training row is a neighbourhood median, so the output does not describe an individual property's current price.
    • Historical bias. The dataset includes the racially derived variable B and the LSTAT "lower status" variable. The model needs them as inputs, and they can encode discriminatory patterns.
    • Do not use these estimates for financial, housing, lending or investment decisions.
